1. THE CLOCK. READ THIS BEFORE ANY TIME IN THIS FILE
----------------------------------------------------
Vikram is on BST, which is UTC+1. His own messages, when he quotes a time, are BST.

Commit subject lines in this estate begin with a 12-digit stamp, e.g. "202609050353". That stamp
is SUPPOSED to be UTC read from `date -u` at the moment of committing. It frequently is not.

Measured across all 82 commits in the window (73 of which carry a stamp):

                          into a UTC field, which is the fault the estate's own cvaa antibody
                          202608301701-monotonic-utc-generations exists to catch)

The two worst are the last two commits of the session:

  globalgrid2050 c1b24e6d  committed 2026-09-05T09:36:49Z, stamped 202609050415  (-322 min)
  claude         0e95028   committed 2026-09-05T09:37:23Z, stamped 202609050415  (-322 min)

Both carry a stamp more than five hours older than the moment they were made. Anyone reading the
subject line alone would place these commits at 04:15 UTC. They are 09:36 UTC.

RULE FOR A FRESH SESSION: never trust a commit's subject stamp. Use `git log --date=iso-strict`
or the committer epoch. `TZ=UTC git log --date=format-local:'%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ'` gives true UTC
on this machine; a bare `--date=iso-strict` prints +01:00 local time and will mislead by an hour.
