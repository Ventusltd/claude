CORRECTIONS TO THE EXPLANATIONS IN THE PASTED RECORD
===================================================
- cache:no-store bypasses the HTTP response cache; it does not itself force a fresh
  DNS lookup, TCP connection and TLS handshake on EVERY visit. Those may be reused.
  A cold connection can pay setup cost. The unbounded dependency is the proven defect.
- Roughly 10 MB here describes decoded JSON, not 10 MB transmitted on the wire. The
  measured project transfer is around 1.18 MB compressed; parsing/DOM costs remain real.
- The quoted 323,801 measurement counts DOM elements via querySelectorAll('*'), not
  every text/comment node. Total DOM nodes would be a different, potentially larger count.
- The earlier race explanation does not explain the measured off-screen button. This
  review independently confirms valid hrefs can coexist with an unreachable target.
- The new tests are not automatically trustworthy because they say 'outcomes'. They
  still need correct interactions, a failing control and coverage of the actual release.
- No iOS memory-crash mechanism, Android success, cold-radio benchmark or full estate
  green result is claimed from Windows browser emulation.
